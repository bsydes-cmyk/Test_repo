# AI Slide Deck Creators: Research Report (April 2026)

**Criteria:** NotebookLM-quality content synthesis from source documents, with fully editable slide output.
**Already tried:** Gamma (not good enough), Canva, NotebookLM (great but not editable).

---

## Top 5 Recommendations (Ranked)

### 1. Skywork AI - Best "NotebookLM but editable"
- **What it does:** Deep-research-powered slide agent. Upload documents or provide prompts and it produces multi-slide narratives with speaker notes and verifiable citations.
- **Editability:** Exports fully editable PPTX files. Supports brand template and color scheme uploads.
- **Why it fits:** DeepResearch with citations - analyzes a wide set of sources and attaches verifiable citations, closest to NotebookLM's intelligence.
- **Pricing:** Freemium; Pro at ~$19.99/month.
- **Links:** [Skywork](https://skywork.ai/) | [NotebookLM vs Skywork comparison](https://www.hubsite365.com/en-ww/crm-pages/notebooklm-slides-are-good-but-skywork-is-next-level-full-comparison.htm)

### 2. Alai - Best for design quality + iteration
- **What it does:** Generates 4 layout options per slide (not just one), so you pick the best visual treatment for each idea. Has an Agent Mode for editing through natural conversation.
- **Editability:** Fully editable with Nano Banana Pro integration for theme-consistent editing.
- **Input:** Supports notes, URLs, screenshots, PDFs, and existing PPTs.
- **Pricing:** Free tier (200 credits); paid from ~$16-20/month.
- **Why it fits:** Strongest visual output quality among all tools tested; iterative refinement via AI conversation.
- **Links:** [Alai](https://getalai.com) | [Alai Review](https://dokie.ai/blog/reviews/alai-review) | [Alai Pricing](https://getalai.com/pricing)

### 3. Plus AI (Google Slides / PowerPoint add-on) - Best for ecosystem integration
- **What it does:** Works inside Google Slides and PowerPoint natively. Upload PDFs, DOCX, TXT files to generate full presentations.
- **Editability:** Output is native Google Slides / PowerPoint - fully editable, shareable, no vendor lock-in.
- **Pricing:** Free 7-day trial; Basic $15/mo, Pro $25/mo (adds document upload + AI images), Team $40/mo.
- **Caveat:** Uses lower-grade language models - solid for business presentations but may struggle with highly technical topics.
- **Links:** [Plus AI](https://plusai.com/blog/best-ai-presentation-makers) | [Plus AI Review](https://work-management.org/productivity-tools/plus-ai-review/)

### 4. WorkPPT - Best for speed + templates
- **What it does:** Converts PDFs, Word docs, images, and ideas into structured editable slides in under 60 seconds. 5,000+ templates across business, education, and marketing.
- **Editability:** Full PPTX export, also PDF and images.
- **Why it fits:** Directly designed to solve the "NotebookLM slides aren't editable" problem.
- **Pricing:** Freemium model.
- **Links:** [WorkPPT](https://ppt.softtooler.com/blog/ai-productivity/notebooklm-alternative/)

### 5. Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint - Best if in the Microsoft ecosystem
- **What it does:** Generates slides from Word documents, prompts, and source files directly inside PowerPoint with proper templates.
- **Editability:** Fully native PowerPoint - the most polished editing experience.
- **Pricing:** Requires Microsoft 365 Copilot license (~$30/user/month).
- **Caveat:** Expensive; requires M365 subscription.

## Key Insight

NotebookLM's slides are rendered as **flat images** - visually impressive but fundamentally locked. Every tool above solves this by exporting editable PPTX or working inside Google Slides/PowerPoint natively.

## Recommendation

For the specific need of "NotebookLM-level intelligence + editable output":
1. **Try Skywork first** - closest to NotebookLM's research depth with citations
2. **Try Alai second** - best visual quality with 4-layout options per slide
3. **Consider Plus AI** if you want to stay in Google Slides / PowerPoint
