## 2. Janet's Economics — The Prescriptive Answer

Janet's stated target: **12.69% total** (11.54% capital + 1.15% raise).

**Recommended path:**

- **Janet's TIC interest = 11.54%** (her $150K capital ÷ total $1.3M project equity portion × the 50% capital bucket math: 23.08% × 50% = 11.54%). This is TIC-clean under §6.08.
- **Janet's 1.15% gap is recovered through her HotelCo consulting agreement.** Contract is arm's-length, FMV, and outside the TIC entirely.
- The contract should be sized so that Janet's after-tax compensation over the hold period equals approximately **1.15% × expected total deal economics**, grossed up because consulting income is ordinary (not capital gain).
- Worked example: if expected total deal economics over 7 years = $4M, then 1.15% = $46,000. After-tax (at Janet's marginal rate ~35%) the gross contract should be ~$71,000 over 7 years, or ~$10,000/year as a base consulting fee plus performance bonuses tied to GUEST-EXPERIENCE metrics that HotelCo can document independently.

**Alternative path (if Janet wants 12.69% strictly from her TIC interest, no consulting contract):**

Janet must increase her capital contribution from $150K to ~$170K. Math:
- Janet's TIC % must = 12.69% / 50% = 25.38% of the 50% capital bucket
- With other capital contributions held at $500K (Single Seed $100K + Gideon $50K + Tiffany $50K + York $100K + Ryan $200K), Janet's contribution X satisfies: X / (X + $500K) = 0.2538
- Solving: X = $170,062
- Total raise becomes $670K (not $650K)

**Recommendation:** Use the consulting-agreement path (lower friction, no recapitalization needed). Save the increased-contribution path as a backup.

## 5. SBA Treatment of This Structure

- **Single EPC** = the TIC pool (JV LLC + Janet + Ryan) under SOP 50 10 8.
- **Single OC** = HotelCo LLC, which leases 100% of the property under a written, subordinated lease with assignment of rents.
- **Co-borrowers / co-mortgagors:** all three TIC owners must sign the mortgage covering the entire fee. The note is a single $575K obligation with joint-and-several liability among the TIC owners.
- **Personal guarantees** (under 13 CFR §120.111(a)(4), 20%+ of EPC OR OC):
  - Single Seed (effective ~24.6% of EPC via 33.62% × 73.08%) — **PG required**
  - Ryan (15.38% of EPC) — **PG required** (he is also flagged in the user's table)
  - Janet (11.54% of EPC) — under 20%, **not required** by the regulation, but the user's table flags her as PG; if she signs a PG voluntarily that's permissible

- **No SBA proceeds touch the $97K acquisition fee** (it's a capital-account credit, not cash).
- **Citizenship/residency** must be confirmed for every direct and indirect owner under Policy Notice 5000-865754.

## 7. Hard Constraints That Are Genuinely Unchangeable

These three rules are physics, not preferences. The structure cannot bend them:

1. **TIC owners cannot share in a curved waterfall.** Rev. Proc. 2002-22 §6.08 requires strict pro-rata. The "no more, no less" mandate is satisfied at the **base case**, but in upside scenarios the JV partners get the promote (which the TICs cannot share in) and in downside scenarios losses fall pro-rata on TICs in a way the JV partners may have protective structures to avoid. This asymmetry is the **price** of §1031 deferral and is unavoidable.

2. **TIC owners cannot operate the hotel.** They can own only the real estate. All hospitality services run through HotelCo.

3. **HotelCo and JV LLC cannot be the same entity.** If JV LLC is on title as a TIC AND operates the hotel, it is simultaneously cotenant + lessee, violating §6.12 and creating §6.13 self-dealing.

Everything else in the user's structure can be preserved. The 7 changes above accomplish that.

## 9. What Still Needs To Be Confirmed Before Closing

These three facts gate the entire structure. Get answers from the user:

1. **Is there a real Operating Company tenant identified?** If yes — proceed. If no — the EPC/OC SBA path doesn't work; restructure as a single OpCo LLC with everyone inside, give up TIC + 1031, file Form D for any non-active passive members.
2. **Are all direct and indirect owners U.S. citizens or U.S. nationals with U.S. residence?** Policy Notice 5000-865754 hard-disqualifies LPRs and most non-citizens from SBA borrower ownership. **Confirm before any other work proceeds.**
3. **Has the deal been pitched as a bundled rental-management program to anyone?** If yes, *Hocking* / *Salameh* convert the offering into a security and Reg D 506(b) filing becomes necessary regardless of the JV's structural defense.
